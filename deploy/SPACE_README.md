# CropShift Earth

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Cairo. How much is a NASA measurement changing at a place, how confident
we are that the change is real, and what it means for farming in the Middle East and North Africa.

This Space is deployed from the team repository with `python run.py space deploy <user>/<space>`.
