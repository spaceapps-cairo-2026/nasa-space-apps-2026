## Pre-thinking per candidate (to discuss tonight)

- **Flame in Freefall:** explicitly AI. Likely shape: retrieval over decades of ISS combustion experiment records + a model of flame behaviour, answering "is this material/configuration a fire risk in microgravity". Risk: dataset format unknown until we read the statement.
- **MODIS/VIIRS Harmonization:** crisp ML task (map one sensor onto the other's scale, then build a consistent fire calendar and flag anomalies). Data is easy via FIRMS. Egypt angle: agricultural burning in the Delta (the "black cloud" season). Risk: looks less flashy unless the anomaly story is strong.
- **Earth Analogs:** similarity search between Earth regions and Moon/Mars sites using terrain, temperature, and spectral features. Egypt angle: Western Desert and Sinai as analog sites. Very demoable map.
- **Field Shift:** decision support for crop rotation under climate risk. Egypt angle: Nile Delta farms, water stress. Overlaps with the 2025 Egyptian winner's theme (farmers), which cuts both ways.
- **Trend Detective:** statistically rigorous trend detection (is a change significant or noise). Strong on Validity, weaker on wow factor.
