# One sentence pitch

For farmers, investors and planners across the Middle East and North Africa who must decide what to grow and where, CropShift Earth uses 25 years of NASA POWER climate data and a crop model trained on where 40 crops grow today to show which changes are real, where, how fast, and which crops will still fit in 2035, checked against NASA's own weather stations.

Short version (for a title slide): **Is your planet really changing? And what can you still grow?**

## Numbers to say out loud (all sourced, see `docs/SUBMISSION.md` and `docs/REFERENCES.md`)
- Egypt, NASA weather stations: **+0.42 °C per decade** since 2001, about **1.7 times** the global rate (+0.25) measured the same way.
- **1 in 6** Egyptian workers works in farming (17.5%, ILO estimate 2025).
- Middle East and North Africa: the **most water-scarce region** in the world (World Bank, 2018).
- Our engine on NASA GISTEMP reproduces global warming: **+0.25 °C per decade**, strong evidence.
- Crop model: **40 crops**, **20 countries**, held-out accuracy **AUC 0.90**; trained without Egypt it still recognizes Egypt's real crops (**AUC 0.93**).
- Sudan: proven cooling in **238** places in the south, proven warming in **26** in the north. One country, opposite trends.
- We found a jump in NASA POWER rain around 2015 (**x2.6**) that NASA's GPCP record does not show (**x1.24**), and handled it.

### 2 minute version (stage pitch only)

## Wording rules for the video and slides
- Say "strong evidence", "moderate evidence", "not proven". Never "no change", never "87% true".
- Future views: always "if current trends continue". Never "will".
- Never claim a cause. Say "consistent with".
- No long dashes in any text on screen.
- English subtitles if anyone speaks Arabic. No names, voices or faces of anyone under 18.

## Judge criteria checklist
- [x] Impact: user + scale stated with a number (hook and benefits above)

- [x] Validity: engine vs NASA GISTEMP, stations vs POWER, the rain step found
- [x] Relevance: every challenge question named in the demo (step 4)
- [ ] Presentation: video rehearsed, under time, English subtitles (team)
